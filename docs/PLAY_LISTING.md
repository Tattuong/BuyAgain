# Google Play listing — BuyAgain

Package: `com.BuyAgainMNG.BuyAgain`

## Title
BuyAgain

## Short description
Buy enough. Nothing extra. Lists, prices, and a calmer shop.

## Full description
BuyAgain is a shopping list for food, household goods, and one store at a time. Keep a few lists, save what you usually buy, and add that whole group again with one tap.

Enter a quantity, a price per each, kg, or liter, and a budget. The spent total stays on screen. Checked items drop to the bottom in large type. When you finish, share the receipt and turn that trip into the next list. No account.

Looks, keep-screen-on, tap sound, and BuyAgain Pro (more than 8 lists) unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and BuyAgain Pro are one-time unlocks.

Remote config: https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N303.json — if disable=1, Play purchase sheets stay hidden. The shop still spends coins.
