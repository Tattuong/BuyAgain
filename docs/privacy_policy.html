Privacy Policy – BuyAgain

Last updated: October 2026

BuyAgain is a private, on-device shopping list. Your lists, usual items, trip totals, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account or upload what you buy.

Data stored on device

  • Shopping lists, item prices, and finished trips
  • Items you save as usual buys
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and BuyAgain Pro

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and BuyAgain Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N303.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
